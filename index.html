📚 Engineering Formulae & Test Rig Specifications

1. Brake Power (BP)

BP = (√3 × Voltage × Current × cos Φ) / 1000 [kW]

Where cos Φ = 1.0 (pure resistive 3-phase load bank). At zero load, BP = 0.0 kW.

2. Total Fuel Consumption (TFC) & Time (t)

TFC = (Xcc × ρf × 3600) / (1000 × t) = 59.04 / t [kg/hr]

Fuel burette volume Xcc = 20 cc, diesel density ρf = 0.82 g/cc.

3. Brake Specific Fuel Consumption (BSFC)

BSFC = (TFC × 1000) / BP [g/kWh]

Represents the mass of fuel burned per kilowatt-hour of useful mechanical work delivered at the engine shaft.

4. Brake Thermal Efficiency (ηbth)

ηbth = (BP × 3600) / (TFC × CV) × 100 [%]

Calorific value of diesel CV = 45,300 kJ/kg.

5. Volumetric Efficiency (ηv) & Air Flow

ha = (ρw / ρair) × (Δhw / 1000) [m air column]
Va = Cd × Ao × √(2 × g × ha) × 3600 [m³/hr]
Vs = 2 × [(π / 4) × D² × L × (N / 2) × 60] [m³/hr]
ηv = (Va / Vs) × 100 [%]

Bore D = 87.5 mm, Stroke L = 110 mm, Orifice do = 20 mm, Cd = 0.62, ρair = 1.176 kg/m³.

6. Mechanical Efficiency (ηm) & Willans Line

Friction Power (FP) = 4.44 kW (Willans line intercept)
Indicated Power (IP) = BP + FP [kW]
ηm = (BP / IP) × 100 [%]
